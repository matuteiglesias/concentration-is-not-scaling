# Scientific scope

This repository contains one closed formal paper.

## Frozen contribution

The paper establishes:

- the identification boundary between concentration and variance
  scaling;
- non-identification even from the complete weight path;
- an exact finite-interval decomposition of variance elasticity;
- signed covariance attribution;
- the component-balance interpretation of diversification.
