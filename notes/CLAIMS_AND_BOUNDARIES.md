# Claims and boundaries

## Formal claims

The paper formally distinguishes concentration levels from aggregate-variance scaling elasticities, provides a finite-interval decomposition, and permits signed covariance contributions.

## Nonclaims

The paper makes no empirical French-export claim, estimates no French-export exponent, and does not identify a causal mechanism in French-export data.

## Convention

All displayed scaling objects concern **variance** elasticities. A standard-deviation elasticity is one half of the corresponding variance elasticity when the variance is positive.

## Evidence status

The deterministic v0.2 artifact verifies algebraic identities only. It is generated evidence in `REVIEW`, not primary empirical evidence.
