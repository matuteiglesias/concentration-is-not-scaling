# Thesis handoff

The current paper now owns the following arguments:

- concentration and variance scaling are different estimands;
- the Gabaix bridge requires homogeneous, independent,
  scale-invariant shocks;
- even the full weight path does not identify the variance path;
- aggregate alpha is generated by diagonal and covariance component
  paths;
- heterogeneous component elasticities produce changing contribution
  shares;
- positive covariance may contribute negatively to variance decay.

Future thesis-derived papers may cite this paper rather than
re-deriving these points.

The broader thesis archive retains:

- French exporter and importer evidence;
- micro shock distributions;
- nonlinear nominal transformations;
- size and sector partitions;
- intensive and extensive margins;
- empirical covariance matrices;
- direct export-alpha reproduction;
- mechanisms explaining the observed empirical exponent.
