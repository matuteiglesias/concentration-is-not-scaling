# Cover letter — PI confirmation required

Dear Editor,

Please consider “Concentration Is Not Scaling: An Exact Decomposition of Aggregate Variance Elasticities” by Matías Iglesias for publication in *Economics Letters*.

The manuscript is a concise formal contribution. It separates concentration from the stochastic bridge required to identify aggregate-volatility scaling: even the complete economic-weight path does not identify variance scaling over broader covariance paths. It then derives an exact finite-interval decomposition into changes in diagonal variance and signed covariance components.

This is not a pedagogical comment. It presents a self-contained identification boundary and exact accounting result. No empirical data are used. The deterministic code and verification artifacts are available in the associated repository, subject to the PI confirming its public availability at submission.
