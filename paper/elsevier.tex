\begin{highlights}
\item Concentration does not identify aggregate-variance scaling.
\item Variance elasticities admit an exact finite-interval decomposition.
\item Signed covariance enters without logarithms of component values.
\item Equal concentration paths can imply different diversification rates.
\item Positive covariance can contribute negatively to variance decay.
\end{highlights}
