The official arXiv license page could not be retrieved from this environment on 2026-07-24, so the final platform wording must be read by the PI before submission.

| Option | Reuse implication | Elsevier compatibility consideration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| arXiv perpetual non-exclusive license | arXiv receives a continuing non-exclusive right to distribute the submitted version; copyright is not transferred by this choice alone. | Common preprint route, but the PI must compare the eventual Elsevier agreement and manuscript-version policy. |
| CC BY 4.0 | Broad reuse and adaptation are permitted with attribution, including commercial reuse. | May be compatible with some publisher policies, but it grants broad downstream reuse and must be checked against the later publishing agreement. |
| CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 | Attribution is required; commercial reuse and distribution of adaptations are restricted. | The requested default for PI consideration; compatibility with any later Elsevier agreement must still be confirmed. |

An arXiv license selection for a submitted version is irrevocable. Different versions may carry different licenses. The recommended default for **PI consideration only** is **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**; this document does not make that choice on the PI's behalf.
